# RKHP-01 Development Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap defines the engineering path for RKHP-01, the first experimental
robotics platform within RKHP.

The roadmap focuses on turning the RKHP architecture into a measurable,
testable, and progressively more capable physical system.

The roadmap is intentionally iterative.

Hardware, software, and intelligence decisions may change as experiments
produce new evidence.

---

# Development Philosophy

RKHP-01 will not be built by attempting to solve everything at once.

Development will progress through increasingly capable stages:

Define
  ↓
Simulate
  ↓
Measure
  ↓
